# Code of conduct

CodeCoreMorph adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct, for everyone who takes part in the project: in issues, pull requests,
discussions and anywhere else the project is represented.

In short: be welcoming, considerate and respectful; disagree about ideas, not people; accept
that maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break these rules, and may
ban people who keep breaking them.

To report behavior that breaks the code of conduct, contact the maintainers privately. Reports
are handled in confidence, and the people involved are told only what they need to know.
Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines: a correction, a warning,
a temporary ban or a permanent ban, depending on what happened.
